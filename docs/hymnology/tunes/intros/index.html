Hymn intros

Hum the opening of a hymn and write it as scale degrees: 1 is do, 2 is re, up to 8 for the high do. The line below draws your melody, and the page finds the hymnal.net hymns that share the tune and tells you who wrote it. You can also tap the staff to add notes.

Try:

Hymn codes, numbers and titles are from hymnal.net, whose hymn pages list each tune as a code of scale degrees (octave ignored, so a typed 8 counts as 1). The tunes listed here were checked one by one against hymnal.net's own hymn-code search; for any other opening, the search link sends you to hymnal.net's full results. "Original tune" means the tune the hymn carries on hymnal.net; "Alternate tune" and "New tune" are hymnal.net's own additional settings. Composer notes are written from public sources, with a link to read more.
