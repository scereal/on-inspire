Build a square wave

Sine waves are smooth. A square wave has sharp corners. Can you build one out of the other?

square wave your sum each sine

Design notes

Learning goal: find out, by experimenting, that a square wave is the sum (4/π)·sin(nt)/n over odd n, and that its overshoot never goes away.

  • The learner experiments before seeing any formula. The recipe comes out of their own slider settings.
  • Each wrong answer gets its own feedback. Misconceptions targeted: "amplitude 1 matches a height of 1," "any extra sine helps," "the amplitudes halve each time," and "more terms remove the overshoot."
  • The gap readout is the root-mean-square distance, so the learner can see why 4/π beats 1.

Built with Claude Code. The amplitudes, gap and overshoot values are checked by automated tests.
