Integration by parts, one step at a time

Seven short problems. Each one tests a single step, so you're practicing the idea rather than grinding through algebra.

The rule: ∫ u dv = uv − ∫ v du

Design notes

Learning goal: use integration by parts reliably by practicing each step on its own: choosing u, finding v, assembling uv − ∫ v du, and checking by differentiating.

  • Each item isolates one step, so a mistake points to exactly one gap.
  • The planted errors are the common ones: the sign of ∫ sin x, differentiating instead of integrating for v, dropping the chain-rule factor, and flipping the minus in the rule.
  • The last item teaches the habit that catches all of them: differentiate your answer.

Built with Claude Code. Every correct answer, planted error and blank is checked symbolically with SymPy in automated tests.
