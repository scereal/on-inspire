Two cups of tea

You have strong tea, water, and two cups that only have four marks. How exact can you get?

Practice more like this

Design notes

Learning goal: reason about strength as a fraction of the whole: parts versus ratios, mixing a mix (serial dilution), and averaging two strengths.

  • The cups hold only 4 marks, so 1/8 looks impossible. That constraint is the puzzle: the only way past it is to dilute something that's already diluted.
  • Misconceptions targeted: reading "1 to 3" as 1/3, believing a cup's size limits its precision, and adding or multiplying strengths instead of averaging them.
  • Every move is one mark, so the learner works with whole parts and never has to measure.
